# Installing DropTech

DropTech is configured as a progressive web app. Deploy the site over HTTPS (GitHub Pages provides HTTPS), then open it in a supported browser and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**. On iPhone and iPad, open the site in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

The app shell is cached by the service worker for repeat visits and offline startup. Product images are cached as they are viewed. Ordering still needs an internet connection to open WhatsApp.
